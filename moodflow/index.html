Moodflow: Mood Tracker — Support

Moodflow is a calm, private mood tracker for iPhone. Check in once a day, tag what shaped your day, and see gentle insights about your patterns.

FAQ

How do I check in?
On the Today tab, pick a mood, optionally tap tags and add a note, then tap Log Mood.
Can I change today's check-in?
Yes. Tap "Change today's check-in" on the Today tab. Moodflow keeps one check-in per day.
How do I delete an entry?
Open the History tab and swipe left on the entry.
Where are the insights?
The Trends tab shows your weekly averages, your mood by weekday, and which tags appear on your best days. Insights appear after a few check-ins.
Is my data backed up or synced?
No. Moodflow stores everything only on your iPhone and never connects to the internet. Deleting the app deletes your data.
