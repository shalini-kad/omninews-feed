Moodflow: Mood Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Summary

Moodflow does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server of its own.

Data we collect

None. Your check-ins (mood, optional tags and optional note) are stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and the app makes no network connections. Deleting an entry removes it; deleting the app removes all of your data.

Health information

Moodflow is a personal journaling tool. It does not read from or write to Apple Health, and it is not a medical device.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no information, no personal information from anyone, including children, is collected, used, or disclosed.

Changes to this policy

If our practices ever change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
