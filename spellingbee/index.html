Spelling Bee Kids: ABC Words — Support

Spelling Bee Kids helps young children learn letters and spell their first words. A friendly voice reads every word and letter aloud, so no reading is needed to play.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feedback? Email shalinikadavill@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

FAQ

How does my child play?
Pick a picture group (First Words, Animals, Fruits or Things), tap a picture, listen to the word being spelled, then tap "My turn" and build the word from letter tiles. Each new word earns a star.
What are the ABC, abc and Aa buttons?
They switch the letter tiles between big letters, little letters, or a matching game that pairs big and little letters.
There is no sound.
Check that the device is not on silent and the volume is up. The voice uses the speech voices built into iOS, so no download or internet connection is needed.
Does it need the internet or an account?
No. Everything works offline with no sign-in.
Are there ads or purchases?
No. The app is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no links out of the app.
