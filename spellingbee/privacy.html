Spelling Bee Kids: ABC Words — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Summary

Spelling Bee Kids does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party code that collects data, and no server of its own.

Data we collect

None. The only thing the app remembers is which words your child has spelled (to show stars) and the chosen letter style. This is stored only on the device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Speech

Words and letters are spoken by the text-to-speech voices built into iOS, on the device. The app does not use the microphone or record any audio.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for young children. Because it collects no information at all, no personal information from children is collected, used, or disclosed, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases, and no links that leave the app.

Changes to this policy

If our practices ever change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
