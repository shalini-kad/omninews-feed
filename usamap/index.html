USA Map Puzzle: 50 States — Support

USA Map Puzzle is a geography game for iPhone: rebuild the map of the United States state by state, and test yourself with state trivia.

FAQ

How do I play Puzzle Mode?
Drag a state from the tray at the bottom onto its outline on the map. Swipe the tray sideways to see more states. A state snaps into place when it is close to the right spot.
How does Trivia Mode work?
Read the question, then drag the state that answers it onto the map. You get three tries and 15 seconds per question; faster answers and streaks score more points.
How do I start over?
Tap the circular arrow in the top-right corner of Puzzle Mode.
Does it need the internet or an account?
No. Everything works offline, with no sign-in, ads or purchases.
