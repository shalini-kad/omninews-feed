USA Map Puzzle: 50 States — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Summary

USA Map Puzzle does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server of its own.

Data we collect

None. Your best puzzle time, best trivia score and longest streak are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no information, no personal information from children is collected, used, or disclosed.

Changes to this policy

If our practices ever change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
